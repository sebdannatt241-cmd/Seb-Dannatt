GRAVEYARD SMASH

Protect your bases. Destroy both enemy outer bases before attacking their Castle. Eliminate every opponent to win.

Read each rule, then swipe the card away or tap Next Rule to continue.

01 / SETUP

Set up your bases

Tap to read

Each player starts with 11 Zombies: 3 in the Left Base, 3 in the Right Base and 5 in the Castle.

02 / ROLL & DRAW

Roll both dice

Tap to read

Each die has 8 possible faces: 2 Zombie, 2 Werewolf, 2 Vampire and 2 Healing. Matching Werewolves deal 2 Smashes; matching Vampires deal 3. Other monster rolls deal 1 Smash.

03 / HEAL OR ATTACK

Healing takes priority

Tap to read

If either die shows Healing, you heal instead of attacking that turn. Each Healing face restores 1 Zombie, so one face heals 1 and two faces heal 2 if space is available. Healing fills your least-full bases first, up to 3 Zombies in each outer base and 5 in the Castle.

04 / ATTACK

Spend your Smashes

Tap to read

Each Smash destroys one enemy Zombie in a legal target base. You must destroy both the West Base and East Base (reduce each to zero Zombies) before you can attack the Castle. Sacrifice attacks are the exception: sacrificing one of your own Zombies lets you strike the Castle even while an outer base survives. Unused Smashes can be saved by passing, up to a maximum bank of 6.

05 / BARRICADE

Defend your bases

Tap to read

Spend 2 of your Zombies to activate a Barricade with 3 charges. It blocks Werewolf and Vampire attacks, but Zombie attacks bypass it. A Barricade cannot be bought while one is already active.

06 / RALLY

Reinforce a weak flank

Tap to read

Once per turn before rolling, move 1 Zombie from your Castle to your less-populated outer base. You need at least 1 Zombie in the Castle to rally.

07 / HORDE SURVIVAL

Survive the waves

Tap to read

In Horde Survival, all human players cooperate against The Horde. Destroy all its base Zombies to clear a wave. Each new wave has stronger bases, and each surviving player earns one extra ability card: Bone Wall adds 2 Barricade charges before rolling, Grave Surge adds 1 Smash during an attack, and Raise Dead restores up to 2 Zombies before rolling.

RULES

Rules complete!

You’re ready to enter the Graveyard.

Castle protection: A Castle cannot be attacked by normal attacks while either outer base still has Zombies. Destroy both the West Base and East Base first. Sacrifice attacks are the exception and can strike the Castle early. Passing saves unused Smashes for your next turn, up to 6 total.

Optional mode — Siege Tactics: select it when setting up a match to add base-specific effects. Destroy the West Base first to shatter the defender’s Barricade; destroy the East Base first to gain +1 Smash immediately. Standard mode remains unchanged.

Win condition: Eliminate every opponent by destroying all their Zombies. The last player or team with Zombies remaining wins.

Offline play: This is a single self-contained HTML file. Download it before you go offline; the game, dice, rules and artwork are built in and do not need an internet connection. On iPhone, Teams or Files previews may block game scripts, so test that your chosen browser opens the downloaded HTML correctly before relying on it offline.

Player details

A forgiving bot that learns to target weakened opponents.

With one player in a versus mode, you face a bot opponent. The bot follows the same dice, Barricade, and outer-bases-before-Castle rules as players.

Team mode: assign players to Team Red or Team Black. For team matches with several teammates, choose 4 or 6 players.

Siege Tactics: the standard Castle lock still applies, but the order of your outer-base attacks matters. Destroy the West Base first to break the defender’s Barricade. Destroy the East Base first to earn +1 Smash immediately. These effects apply only in this mode.

Horde Survival: cooperate against an enemy Horde. Each cleared wave grows stronger and rewards surviving players with extra ability cards. Choose 1, 2, 4, or 6 human players.

Boss Battle: cooperate through escalating waves. A powerful boss appears every 5th wave with triple base health. Clear the boss wave to earn ability cards. Choose 1, 2, 4, or 6 human players.
